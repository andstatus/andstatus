# AndStatus app Child Safety Standards policy

## Standards
AndStatus takes seriously the safety of children
and is committed to working to keep our app
free of child sexual abuse and exploitation, according to the Google Play 
[Child Safety Standards Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9878809?visit_id=639192140563690402-2584491741&rd=1#child_safety).

AndStatus app prohibits its use for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

## In-App Mechanism for User Feedback
Our app has in-App Mechanism for User Feedback. in the Main menu navigate to Settings
-> Information -> Contact Developer menu item. You will be forwarded to the Issues
section of our app public repository. Please search for existing issue
and raise a new issue if needed.

## Addressing CSAM
AndStatus app team will take appropriate action,
including but not limited to removing CSAM, after obtaining actual knowledge of it, 
in accordance with published standards and relevant laws.

## Compliance with Child Safety Laws
Our app complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations,
including but not limited to, having a process in place to report confirmed CSAM
to the [relevant regional authority](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/148666).

## Child Safety Point of Contact
Email address: andstatus@gmail.com - is a designated point of contact
to receive potential notifications about CSAE content found in this app.
We will enforce and review procedures and take action if required.
